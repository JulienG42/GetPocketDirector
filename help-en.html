Getting started

Compatibility, navigation, and basic settings to help you get started.

What is Pocket Director for?

Pocket Director brings shoot preparation into one place: weather, light, white balance, projects, equipment, checklists, shot lists, and administrative documents.

Which devices support Pocket Director?

Pocket Director is available on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 18 or later, and on Mac with macOS 14 or later.

How do I navigate the app?

On iPhone and iPad, use the Home, Weather, Shoots, Preparation, and Documents tabs. On Mac, these sections are available from the sidebar.

Does the app work without an Internet connection?

Yes. Projects, checklists, shot lists, and documents remain available offline. An Internet connection is required for weather, place search, and iCloud synchronization.

Can I use the app without allowing location access?

Yes. You can manually search for a city or address on the Weather page.

How do I set a default location?

Display a location on the Weather page, then use the pin button. This location will be used automatically the next time you open the app.

Weather and light

Understand forecasts, natural-light times, and camera recommendations.

Where does the weather data come from?

Weather forecasts and astronomical information are provided by Open-Meteo.

What does the Weather page show?

Temperature, humidity, cloud cover, sunrise and sunset, lighting conditions, and a white balance recommendation.

How do I view another location?

Enter a city or address in the search field, then select the location you want.

How do I return to my current location?

Use the location button next to the search field.

What is the time slider for?

It lets you preview weather and lighting conditions at different times of the day.

What does the kelvin value mean?

It is a white balance recommendation for your camera. Use it as a starting point for your on-location settings.

Do the recommendations replace an on-location measurement?

No. Actual conditions may vary depending on the light sources present at the shoot location. Checking conditions on location is still recommended.

How do I view golden hours and blue hours?

Use the “Golden hour” button on the Weather page to display the recommended times.

What is the ND filter recommendation for?

It indicates whether using an ND filter may be useful based on the observed lighting conditions.

Why is the weather not loading?

Check your Internet connection and try again. You can also manually search for another city.

Shoots

Create, organize, and update your shoot projects.

How do I create a shoot?

Open the Shoots tab, then choose “New project.” Enter the information you want to include.

Can I create a shoot without a date?

Yes. The date is optional.

How do I edit a shoot?

Open its details, then select “Edit.”

How do I add shots to capture?

From the project details, open the Shot list section, then add the shots you want.

How do I link a checklist to a shoot?

Select an existing preparation when creating or editing the project.

Can I add a shoot to the Calendar app?

Yes. If the project has a date, you can add it to the Calendar app from its details.

Do project changes automatically update the Calendar event?

No. Changes made in Pocket Director do not automatically update an event that has already been created in Calendar.

What is removed when I delete a project?

Deleting removes the project and its associated information. Documents remain available in the Documents library.

Preparation and equipment

Build your equipment library and prepare reusable checklists.

What is the difference between “My equipment” and a checklist?

My equipment represents the gear you regularly use. A checklist lets you select only the items needed for a specific shoot.

How do I save my equipment?

In Preparation, add a new item, then enter its name, category, and quantity.

Can I add tasks that are not equipment?

Yes. A checklist can also include actions to complete before a shoot.

How do I mark an item as ready?

Tap or click the circle in front of its name to mark it as ready.

Can I reuse a checklist?

Yes. A checklist can be duplicated and used as the starting point for another shoot.

What happens if I edit an item in My equipment?

Checklists that use this equipment are updated automatically.

What happens if I delete an item from My equipment?

It is removed from every checklist where it was used.

Documents

Track administrative documents and files that are useful for your shoots.

Which documents can I track?

Filming permits, authorizations, image releases, contracts, insurance, call sheets, and any other document useful for preparation.

Can I link a document to a shoot?

Yes. Each document can be linked to a specific project.

Which files can I attach?

PDF and Microsoft Word files (.doc and .docx) are supported.

How do I attach a file?

Use the “Attach” action on iPhone and iPad, or drag and drop the file on Mac.

Can I attach multiple files to the same document?

No. Each document can have one attachment at a time.

How do I retrieve an attachment?

Use the share action on iPhone and iPad or the download option on Mac.

Are document templates available?

Yes. Several templates are available on Mac to speed up the administrative preparation of a shoot.

Magic Director

Use the local preparation assistant powered by Apple Intelligence.

What is Magic Director?

Magic Director is a preparation assistant that can suggest ideas, angles, alternatives, and points to watch based on your shoot.

Are requests sent to an external server?

No. Magic Director runs directly on your device using Apple Intelligence.

Why is Magic Director not showing?

This feature requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence and a compatible version of iOS, iPadOS, or macOS.

Which information does it use?

Depending on the context, Magic Director may use your question, the displayed location, the weather, your shoots, or the selected checklist.

Can I access my previous conversations?

Yes. Your conversation history is available from the Home screen.

Can I turn off Magic Director?

Yes. This feature can be disabled in the app settings.

Can Magic Director make mistakes?

Yes. Generated suggestions are intended to assist with shoot preparation and do not replace the judgment of the director or technical crew.

Data and synchronization

Understand local storage, iCloud, and the management of your information.

Which data is synchronized with iCloud?

Shoots, shot lists, preparations, equipment, documents, and Magic Director history can be synchronized through iCloud.

Is my data lost if iCloud is temporarily unavailable?

No. A local copy remains stored on your device.

Can I turn off iCloud synchronization?

Yes. It can be disabled in the app settings.

Is my data sold or analyzed?

No. Pocket Director contains no advertising system and does not sell user data.

What is the difference between resetting settings and deleting all data?

Resetting restores only the app preferences. Deleting data erases your saved information.

Accessibility and support

Adapt the app to your preferences and contact support.

How do I change the language or units?

Pocket Director supports multiple languages as well as 12-hour / 24-hour time and °C / °F formats. These options are available in Settings.

How do I reduce visual effects?

The accessibility settings let you reduce animations and certain visual effects.

How do I report a problem?

Use the “Report a problem” option in the app settings to contact support.
